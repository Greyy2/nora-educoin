## (b) Findings

1. **Every major exchange/broker now ships an AI trading agent, free, to drive trading volume — none shows a standalone AI price.** Bitget (1M+ AI traders, $1.2B AI volume, May 2026 — fetched), Bybit AI (Sep 9 2026, 80M-user surface), Robinhood Cortex, eToro Tori, Coinbase for Agents (Jun 2026; stocks/ETFs Sep 2026), Binance Agent OS (Aug 20 2026), Kraken agentic app (Jul 2026), Webull MCP (Apr–Jun 2026). A small team cannot compete as "the exchange assistant"; distribution and fee capture sit with the venue. Sources: rows 1–12.
2. **Venues are opening agent rails (MCP/API/skills) — this is the entry point for a small team.** Bybit AI Skills (253 endpoints), Binance Agent OS (MCP, subaccount-scoped permissions), Coinbase for Agents (MCP + x402 pay-per-call for paywalled research/data), Webull MCP. A third-party agent/strategy product can run on top of these rails without custody risk, and x402 creates a pay-per-query channel for research agents. Sources: rows 5, 8, 9, 12.
3. **Biggest verified "user pays" evidence is bundled or consumer-finance, not pure trading AI:** Cleo ~$400M ARR (May 2026, ~1.1M payers in 2025); Robinhood Gold 4.8M subs at $5/mo with Cortex as a headline perk (Q2 2026 IR) ≈ $288M/yr run-rate from Gold fees alone (my arithmetic: 4.8M × $5 × 12; excludes annual-plan discounts). AI is used to sell a bundle, not sold alone.
4. **Automation of *rules the user understands* monetizes and gets acquired:** Composer — $215M+ automated trades/day, bought by SoFi (Jun 23 2026); SoFi's own PR frames it as AI that builds rules-based strategies rather than AI making continuous decisions. Autopilot (copy "smart money" portfolios) — ~$750M+ AUM, 80k+ paid subs (secondary, conflicting figures). Pattern: retail pays for "set it and forget it" with transparent logic and a brokerage partner.
5. **Price bands retail traders actually pay (2026):**
   - Bundled consumer AI: $5–15/mo (Robinhood Gold $5; Rocket Money Premium+ $15; Monarch ≤$199/yr).
   - AI stock-scoring/signals: ~$16–29/mo (Danelfin, Kavout); Tickeron cheaper entry.
   - Crypto bot SaaS: $15–129/mo (3Commas $15/$40/$110; Cryptohopper $29/$69/$129).
   - Pro day-trader AI scanners: $54–254/mo (TrendSpider ~$54–82; Trade Ideas $89–254, Holly AI in the $178–254 tier).
6. **Crypto grid/DCA bots are commoditized:** Pionex gives 16 bots free and monetizes a 0.05% fee (2M+ users, $60B/mo volume claimed). A new paid "bot" without an edge story faces a free exchange-native substitute (Pionex, Bitget, Bybit bots).
7. **Prediction markets: bots dominate flow, but consumer agent products are fragile.** Hubble: 3.7% of users produce 37.44% of Polymarket volume; Bloomberg (Apr 2026, via secondary) found bots win on speed/entry price, not forecasting. Olas Polystrat was the flagship consumer AI agent (33% of agents profitable vs ~16% of humans) yet went to maintenance mode on 2026-05-05 after Polymarket changes broke self-custody — platform-dependency risk. Kalshi publishes no bot share; free API, 30+ OSS bots.
8. **AI-run on-chain vaults are small:** Hyperliquid HLP (protocol, not LLM) TVL fell to $182.8M (Sep 27 2026) from ~$269M (Jun); top leader vaults are $0.8–2.9M TVL. No evidence found this run of any LLM-run vault at scale.
9. Implication for the decision (analysis, not evidence): the clearest paying pattern for a small team is a **subscription copilot/strategy layer ($15–60/mo) that plugs into exchange agent rails (MCP/API) and sells transparent automated strategies, copy/"follow" portfolios, or research digests**, with pay-per-call (x402) as a secondary channel. Avoid competing head-on with free exchange assistants and free grid bots.
