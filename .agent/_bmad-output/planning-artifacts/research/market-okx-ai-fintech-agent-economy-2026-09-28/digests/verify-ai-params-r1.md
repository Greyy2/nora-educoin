## Pattern
The five big exchanges (Binance, OKX, Pionex, Bitget, Bybit) all offer "AI" or auto parameters. Each is a one-shot recommendation at bot creation, fit to a trailing backtest window (mostly 7 days, sometimes 20/30/90/180 days) using volatility or Bollinger/ATR-type statistics. None of the pages I read says the range or grid count is re-derived while the bot runs. Some bots have separate trailing or auto-shift features, but I did not verify those in this run.

## Products that diagnose loss causes or adapt by regime
- **Loss diagnosis (which parameter vs which regime caused the loss): none found.** 3Commas' primary page has no loss-explanation feature. Pionex and Binance show backtest max drawdown or 7-day drawdown only. Coinrule offers state-change logs for manual review, not automated attribution.
- **Adapt by regime:**
  - Cryptohopper Algorithm Intelligence switches strategies by market condition.
  - Coinrule Market Regime Detection toggles rules or bots by user-defined ADX/MA/ATR states.
  - A 3Commas "AI Optimizer" with nightly re-tuning appears only in a third-party blog. It is unverified.
  - No exchange-native grid/DCA bot was found that re-tunes its parameters by regime while running.
- Caveat: no Bybit, OKX, Pionex or Bitget primary page was fully fetched, so a newer adaptive feature there cannot be ruled out.
