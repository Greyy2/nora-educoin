### Big-player moves
- **Stripe / Tempo:** Tempo, the payments L1 built by Stripe and Paradigm, launched on mainnet on 2026-03-18 together with Stripe's Machine Payments Protocol (MPP), an open standard for agent-to-service micropayments and recurring payments. A payments directory lists more than 100 services. Named partners include Anthropic, OpenAI, DoorDash, Mastercard, Nubank, Revolut, Shopify and Standard Chartered | https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/03/18/stripe-led-payments-blockchain-tempo-goes-live-with-protocol-for-ai-agents | CoinDesk | 2026-03-18 | accessed 2026-09-28 | H (corroborated by The Defiant and Yahoo Finance) | launch
- **Coinbase x402, usage:** Chainalysis reports more than 100M cumulative x402 transactions through Q1 2026, mostly on Base. Growth surged in Q4 2025 and then "moderated in early 2026". Transactions of $1 or more rose from 49% of volume (early 2025) to 95% (early 2026). Tester-to-payer conversion improved 4x over six months, and week-over-week wallet retention is trending up. Early volume was inflated by meme-token mints (for example PING, 1 USDC per mint) | https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/x402-agentic-payments-adoption/ | Chainalysis (primary analytics) | 2026-06-03 | accessed 2026-09-28 | H | metric
- **x402, demand reality check:** about 131k transactions and about $28k of volume per day, with an average payment of about $0.20. The February 2026 peak day was 3.8M transactions and about $2M. Artemis estimates about 50% of transactions are "gamified" (self-dealing or wash). Artemis quote: "The x402 'agent payments' boom is still mostly a mirage" | https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/03/11/coinbase-backed-ai-payments-protocol-wants-to-fix-micropayment-but-demand-is-just-not-there-yet | CoinDesk (citing Artemis) | 2026-03-11 | accessed 2026-09-28 | H | metric

## (c) Heating vs cooling

**Heating (capital and attention rising):**
1. **Agent payment rails and protocols run by incumbents.** Stripe Tempo mainnet and MPP (March 2026), Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines with 30+ partners including OKX and Coinbase (June 2026), Visa Intelligent Commerce Connect (April 2026) and Visa-OpenAI (June 2026). Every major network shipped an agent-payment product in the first half of 2026.
2. **Trust, guardrail and identity layers for agent money** (spending limits, mandates, agent KYC and identity, reputation). Evidence: Catena's $30M round and charter application, Kite AIR, AP2 mandates, OKX AI's portable on-chain reputation, Mastercard's identity and spending controls. A secondary headline ("'Control the Agents' won the summer", dreaming.press, August 2026, not fetched) points the same way.
3. **Stablecoin-settled agent payments.** Natural, Catena and Kite are stablecoin-native. Mastercard supports stablecoin settlement, and Tempo is a stablecoin L1. Crypto VC share going to AI x crypto rose from 18% to 40% year on year (Binance Research via FXStreet). *The GENIUS Act as a catalyst was not verified this run (see e).*
4. **Agent-to-agent marketplaces run by exchanges.** OKX AI beta has 2,700+ active agents and 20k tasks by August 2026. This is early and small, but it has distribution (150M users).
5. **Vertical finance agents with revenue** (financial analysis, back-office) draw larger rounds, though evidence is weak (Hebbia, unverified).

**Cooling or overhyped:**
1. **Crypto AI-agent tokens.** Most are 70-90% off their highs, VIRTUAL fell from about $5B at peak to about $0.46B, and the sector is worth about $17-25B. VC is rotating toward revenue-backed businesses. Strong evidence.
2. **Pure x402 micropayment volume as a traction metric.** Real demand is small: about $28k per day in March 2026, about 50% gamified according to Artemis, and a February peak driven by meme mints. There are positive quality signals (95% of volume in transactions of $1 or more, 4x conversion, rising retention), but absolute organic volume remains tiny. Headline transaction counts overstate demand.
3. **In-chat consumer checkout** (ChatGPT Instant Checkout). One source reports that OpenAI scaled it back in March 2026. Low confidence; needs verification.
